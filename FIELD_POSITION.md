# Field quadrants and preview

In Farming, choose Upper Left, Upper Right, Lower Left or Lower Right under Field Position. Enable Farm Near Position to apply it. Show Farming Area displays a local testing overlay, even when farming is off: white grid lines split the usable field into four labelled quarters, and green shading/borders show the selected quarter.

Quadrants now use the full field footprint with a half-stud outer safety margin. Idle gathering sweeps alternating rows at most three studs apart, including all four corners. Collection interruptions preserve the sweep target; hazards can skip blocked waypoints until the next pass. Priority targets can delay the sweep. The preview matches these expanded bounds. Legacy positions retain their old margins. Left/Right keep the script's existing long-axis orientation; Upper/Lower split the other axis. These directions stay fixed to the field rather than changing when the camera turns. Existing Left, Right and Center behavior is retained; the rectangular preview is for the four new quadrant choices.

Normal gathering targets, Precision upkeep, blooms, petals and roaming use the chosen bounds. Mark avoidance navigation is clipped to the quarter, and triangulation uses that quarter's corners. Quadrants take precedence over the normal walking pattern while Farm Near Position is enabled. Travel into a quarter can cross its boundary, and combo catching, showers, conversion and other travel/task overrides retain their existing control; the overlay represents the ordinary farming area rather than a physical wall.

Both settings save through normal configuration. The overlay uses 15 anchored, non-colliding/non-touching/non-queryable local parts with four labels and one managed 5 Hz update listener. Parts are reused during updates and destroyed on disable/unload. It does not change field collision or server state.

Geometry, original side orientation, target eligibility, 1,000 roaming samples, clipped graph links, preview geometry, field changes while stopped/running, repeated updates and enable/disable cleanup are tested with the source-derived Luau fixture. Appearance and full farming movement still need live Roblox verification.

Prevent Field Edge Drift (enabled by default and saved in config) adds four invisible local collision walls with their inner faces three studs outside the active field footprint while gathering. Walls are reused, removed immediately before wrapped travel, and removed on the next heartbeat for other activity changes, combo/shower control, death, or leaving the field. Disabling the toggle or unloading destroys them. Live collision behavior still needs Roblox verification.

Ignore Outside Quadrant is a saved optional switch, off by default. It requires autofarm, Farm Near Position, and a quadrant selection. Collection targets use the selected Farm Field (field1) as the fixed home rectangle, including meteors, Token Links, combos and shower stars. Shower contact offsets are clipped to the rectangle. Scheduled conversion, combat and toy travel are retained; this is a collection filter rather than a travel lock.

Idle Field Loop is a saved optional switch, off by default. When ordinary gathering has no collection target, it follows a rounded perimeter loop scaled to the field or selected quadrant, inset by up to two studs. Priority collection interrupts it; the loop resumes its waypoint afterward. It overrides the configured walking pattern while enabled. Disabled retains the existing sweep/random idle behavior. Current hazards and legacy position range remain respected.

Stump Field has a custom Idle Field Loop: each selected quadrant follows two straight inner sides joined by a broad quarter-ellipse, cutting across the outer corner as in the green reference. It mirrors automatically for all four quadrants. Whole-field Stump farming uses an ellipse. Other fields retain the rounded rectangular loop. The existing Idle Field Loop switch controls both routes.

Idle loops use continuous Humanoid:Move direction updates on Heartbeat and after player controls on RenderStep. The added idle density was removed after live movement problems. Collection and travel release the idle driver. Auto Sprinkler uses a 1.5-stud arrival tolerance; lower tiers jump before every center placement (Basic 1, Silver 2, Golden 3, Diamond 4), verifying airborne state with bounded waits. Supreme Saturator places once at center without jumping.

Idle movement now temporarily disables the default PlayerModule controls for the whole idle session, preventing zero-input updates from overwriting directional movement. Ordinary farm ticks reuse the session. Collection/travel releases it, removes the render binding, and restores controls only if they were enabled before idle began. Source-derived tests simulate 120 frames and verify uninterrupted direction plus control restoration. Live movement remains subject to verification in Roblox.

When Idle Field Loop and Farm Near Position select a quadrant, Prevent Field Edge Drift now surrounds that quadrant with four connected invisible walls, including its inner boundaries. The three-stud clearance outside the farming bounds remains. The same guard folder is removed for combo/shower control, travel, stopping and death. Switching quadrants rebuilds the four walls once; normal updates reuse them. Idle waypoint handoffs now use a small speed-based lead (2–4 studs) to start turns before the exact corner.

Stump update: the quadrant footprint now clips the inner pointed corner and pulls the outer curve inward to match the green reference. Route points, collection eligibility, shaded preview and temporary polygon barriers share this footprint. Stump previews hide the old whole-quadrant grid and its empty corner; other fields keep their existing visuals. The local preview reuses 51 non-physical parts (32 conservative fill strips and 19 boundary segments), while collision guards use 19 separate reusable-lifetime edges and the normal activity cleanup.

Stump Idle: Smileys Only is saved and off by default. With Idle Field Loop active in Stump Field it permits only eligible duped smileys to interrupt collection movement; ordinary tokens, petals, precision, blooms, meteors, combos and showers do not interrupt it. Turning it off restores normal collection priorities. Scheduled tasks and conversion can still run.

Barrier entry update: barriers do not arm around a player outside the selected footprint. With Idle Field Loop on, they require an active idle movement session and disappear when it yields. After entry they retain the usual travel/combo/shower cleanup. Idle Pattern Density is now a separate saved switch, enabled by default, applying temporary 10x root density only while driving the pattern. Idle Pattern 140 PMS is separately saved and off by default; when enabled it sets WalkSpeed to 140 only during idle movement. Original physics and owned speed values restore on release.

Exclusive mode update: Stump Idle: Smileys Only now allows combos as well as smileys. Combo team movement can leave the footprint and retains its usual ownership rules. With this mode enabled and Stump selected as Farm Field, the autofarm loop bypasses all other tasks and collection categories, including toys, planters, combat, meteors/showers and conversion. Field entry remains available to reach the idle route. Turning the toggle off restores ordinary automation.

Stump footprint adjustment: shifted up to 1.25 studs outward along both quadrant axes and increased the inner diagonal cut from 38% to 48% of the radii for a moderately thinner band. The same polygon drives the route, preview, target clipping and barriers. Other fields are unchanged.
