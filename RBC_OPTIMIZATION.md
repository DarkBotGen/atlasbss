# RBC runtime improvements

Relaunch Atlas+ with the usual latest-revision loader, open **RBC → Robo Bear Challenge**, and click **Apply Optimized RBC Settings**. This saves Precision upkeep from round 1, nearby buff upkeep, mob prediction and automatic sprinklers. It disables Field Idle: Smileys & Combos Only so that mode cannot suppress RBC tasks. Your preset-specific bee rankings are preserved. Give Precise bees an appropriate ranking: Precision cannot be generated without the relevant bee ability being available.

## Field travel and movement

- RBC validates configured task fields and falls back to an accessible field for the pollen color. Missing selections no longer become nil field indexes.
- Field detection reuses its raycast parameters and queries downward from above the sample point, including elevated arrival points. Field entry allows a bounded settling interval and retries without dereferencing a missing player field.
- A missing navigation graph route during RBC uses the existing protected tween travel instead of intentionally killing the player. This can briefly use tween movement even when the configured graph route failed.
- Landed characters do not wait as though they were still falling. Character getters handle the interval before a replacement character exists.
- Travel movers, collision snapshots and both travel listeners are cleaned up on normal completion, cancellation and death, independently of the selected movement setting. Original collision values are restored. Character parts are scanned once per tween and new parts are observed through one temporary listener.

## Mob avoidance and buffs

- Safe roaming paths are accepted; the inverted hazard check was corrected.
- Live Cogmower, Gold Cogmower, Cogturret, Mechsquito, Mega Mechsquito and regular monster models remain available as the fallback. Internal flying-entity prediction is protected against missing or changed internals and cached briefly instead of repeatedly requiring modules in the gathering loop.
- Cog saws have a managed event listener, bounded lifetimes and a 64-record cap. Short and stationary attack paths include their endpoints. Hazard maps refresh at most every 0.1 seconds and contain at most 256 samples, including a short projection of moving threats.
- Collection and Precision movement check refreshed hazards. When inside danger, the gatherer looks for an escape destination inside the field before continuing collection. This is best-effort client avoidance, not a guarantee against damage.
- RBC Precision upkeep also runs with ordinary autofarm disabled or while fighting cogmowers. Purple marks no longer bypass mob avoidance during RBC. Once Precision is healthy, urgent nearby buffs can interrupt mark collection.
- Prioritize Buff Upkeep targets missing or expiring Haste, Focus, Melody, Baby Love, color boosts, Inspire and Bear Morph, plus nearby Token Links. Duplicate tokens continue to respect the allowlist. Buff maintenance can only collect abilities actually generated by the current bees.
- Equal-priority bee choices now use explicit gifted, mutation, beequip, level and button-index comparisons. The previous mutation and beequip fields were swapped.

## Memory and CPU work

- RBC menu/round transitions use an iterative driver instead of recursive calls retaining previous quest arrays and GUI references.
- Hazard, buff and path-query caches retain one bounded current snapshot and are cleared on teardown. Turning off the last RBC/party watcher clears its tracked mob sets and disconnects all three watcher listeners.
- Path raycasts reference the map folders directly instead of allocating a fresh Decorations descendant list for every route.
- The avoidance grid measures distance relative to each node correctly. Its redundant quadratic perimeter-link pass was removed, and immutable edge metadata is shared rather than allocated per edge.

## Validation and limits

The Lua source compiles. Source-derived local simulations cover missing fields, raw-vector and zero-length hazard paths, broken internal tracking fallback, emergency escape, 1,000 projectile lifetimes, repeated watcher setup and teardown, 10,000 RBC transitions, grid connectivity against the previous implementation, safe buff/Precision selection, 100 completed tweens, cancelled/dead tween cleanup, collision restoration and landing behavior. Existing combo, field/quadrant and bee-priority regressions are also checked.

These checks do not measure Roblox process memory or prove every long-session leak is eliminated. Live RBC rounds under real mob timing, latency and multi-instance load remain necessary to establish performance and damage rates. No coordinator/server changes are required.
