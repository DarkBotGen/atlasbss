# Atlas+ Safeshower

The previous shower tween/priority selector has been replaced with the Safeshower code supplied in `message (7).txt`. The integration retains its ownership detection, observed light ordering, movement algorithm, world labels and movement handling.

## Operation

- Enable **Farm Showers** and autofarm. The chaser runs only during gathering in the current field; conversion, travel outside that field, stopping, or death disables it. During gathering, the detector watches for your Star Shower passive popup (`5101364168`) and a matching Star Shower Guide. Overlapping/ambiguous owners are rejected rather than guessed.
- Once your guide is accepted, shower handling takes priority over normal gathering, regardless of gathering order. Active combo catches take priority over showers under the Coconut Team role rules. Main farming, standalone movement, facing, speed overrides, auto-dig and other named movement jobs are suspended. Existing character movement tweens and travel constraints are canceled.
- The supplied chaser starts toward light 1 with a 20% WalkSpeed boost (capped at 210), with departure still planned from the live starting WalkSpeed, aiming to arrive 500ms before the estimated expiry. Subsequent transitions use its live twice-ping lead and 210 PMS cap.
- Light numbers follow observed first appearance. Actual visual removal advances the sequence, with the supplied ping-based early transition when a next light is known. There is no invented fixed 0.3-second spawn schedule.
- Near a light, it brakes and uses the supplied small circular path on the edge facing the next light. It uses `Humanoid:Move`, WalkSpeed and velocity braking, with **no shower tween or density change**.
- On completion or cancellation, the engine restores speed, camera and controls. Paused jobs resume when their settings still permit them. Stop Everything and unloading prevent automatic resumption.
## Controls

Farm Showers controls the integrated chaser. The standalone Safeshower panel, arming controls, graph and report UI have been removed. Numbered world labels and the green TARGET highlight remain. Turning Farm Showers off or using Stop Everything cancels the current run.

## Integration safeguards

Required guide/light visuals are allowed through Hide Particles. Anti-lag leaves the physical WarningDisk and Star Shower Guide properties intact so the supplied detector can recognize their material, size and color.

Only active named movement workers retain restart metadata; it is removed on completion/cancellation. While a shower owns movement, new movement-worker requests are queued instead of starting competing loops. Popup property listeners are disconnected and unregistered when their instances leave the scene. The removed panel no longer retains run or event history. Full cleanup also runs after a partial startup failure.

## Validation and limits

Full-script compilation and isolated tests passed for own-popup/guide recognition, rejecting another player's shower, suspending/resuming movement workers, blocking competing restarts, canceling an existing movement tween, ten observed light removals, restoring speed/input/camera, 500 destroyed popup lifecycles, unloading mid-shower and startup-failure cleanup. Existing priority-config, memory, petal, bloom, smiley, allowlist and bear-timing regressions also passed.

These tests use an engine mock. No live 9–10x catch rate has been measured. The supplied timing estimates and visual observations are not server confirmation of collection; network delays, replication ordering and client stalls can still affect results.

## Saved priorities

The prior config fix remains: `prioritization.order` replaces the empty default during config loading, preserving the saved ordering and enabled flag. Showers now override that ordering while active; the order still applies to other gathering.

Config saves also remember the active preset for the current account. Loading a config launches this Atlas+ release, and startup preserves an existing default config.

Assigned/local combo catches override other gathering targets, including showers. Catch success still depends on distance, timing and latency.

Main-owned combos use local role rules: the two spawning alts focus landings in Team Field (Pepper Patch), and the three outside catchers focus landings in Outside Catch Field (default Coconut Field). Each configured catcher in the appropriate group commits; there is no nearest-catcher exclusion. The main always catches its private 0x drop. It then joins every N shared drops that remain in the same field (default 3, adjustable from 2–10 with Main Help Every N Shared Drops). The shared-drop counter resets after field changes and at a new private 0x drop; the main resumes ordinary farming on intervening drops. Helpers yield during the main's scheduled help drop. The interval must match on all clients and is included in Copy Team Code.

For alt-owned combos with an explicit numeric FX Combo value, 0x is private to the spawner, odd stacks (1x, 3x, 5x...) are reserved for the main alone, and even stacks belong to the spawner with outside helpers allowed in Outside Catch Field. Main and spawning-alt turns are selected locally without an HTTP assignment delay. Other accounts reject stale relay jobs that conflict with the observed turn. Nearby non-main accounts move 18 studs clear of reserved main landings and hold that clearance until the landing expires or advances. If stack metadata is absent, the existing coordinator assignment behavior remains the fallback.

Combo movement uses a fast continuous approach (up to at least 180 PMS, with braking near the center). During an actual combo session only, the character root's density is multiplied by ten (clamped to Roblox's property limits) and Massless is disabled. The exact original CustomPhysicalProperties and Massless values restore on completion, target changes, interruption or unload. No density changes apply to shower or normal gathering movement. Cross-field combo travel uses the navigation graph with cancellation/expiry checks and does not invoke the normal travel helper's character-reset failure behavior. Existing coordinator software is unchanged.

Combo tracking uses FallingCoconut landing position and duration. KickedComboCoconut is only a kick notification in the supplied live trace (Pos1, Rad, Points), so it does not create a new destination; the following FallingCoconut does. Legacy follow-ups with an explicit destination and duration remain supported. A bounded 30-second chain-owner cache preserves the spawning account across target changes; each new drop receives a distinct local key, and the preceding landing is retired when the next bounce is observed. The UI Tracking line exposes missing landing data. Main, spawning-alt and outside-catcher regressions cover consecutive drops, repeated IDs/positions and target changes. The sanitized live main-account trace is replayed in the regression fixture, alongside synthetic role and cleanup cases.

Combo landing detection also uses Atlas's original `WarningDisk` + `GuiAttach` detector, independently of the regular Farm Combo Coconuts toggle. Pending disks are checked by the existing combo tick, including delayed GuiAttach creation, and live disk positions correct matching FX targets. Until owner FX arrives, configured accounts catch unlabelled disks locally in their assigned field (main/on-field alts in Team Field; outside catchers in Outside Catch Field). This fallback deliberately favors saving an unlabelled first drop over enforcing owner alternation before ownership is known. It never invents an owner or sends an unknown-owner observation to the relay. Once linked, the existing owner/turn rules apply. Removed disks and stale pending entries are released; unload clears the disk registry. No coordinator update is required.

The team controller consumes the original `tracked.combococonuts` collection as well, including anti-lag virtual entries (`Name=true`, position, points, expiry). The original FX classification enables combo targets when either Farm Combo Coconuts or team/fast mode is enabled. Therefore the regular farming toggle can stay off. Physical and virtual targets share the same field fallback, owner matching, movement priority, and cleanup path.

Combo movement is also applied from the existing Heartbeat tick, so it does not depend exclusively on render callbacks when rendering is disabled or throttled. If the original short field raycast misses, combo-only field lookup checks field-part horizontal bounds and selects the closest matching height. Coconut Team diagnostics report separate FX/Atlas target counts and the local account role; idle/error reasons update even before a movement session starts. These changes pass mock regressions, including movement with render callbacks absent; the reported live misses still require in-game verification.

Team-selected catches now run through the original Atlas `collectObject` walk/hold routine in one cancellable `comboAtlasCatch` worker. The team controller supplies its live target and speed, while the collector bypasses ordinary gathering preemption only for that active combo session. Changing targets, stopping, expiry, and unload cancel the worker and restore movement/density. Outside catchers also select alt-owned combos directly in Outside Catch Field; explicit stack counts select spawning-alt/main turns locally, while coordinator assignment remains the fallback when counts are absent. The main helps with its own shared combo only at the configured same-field interval. Regression coverage executes the production collector with Farm Combo Coconuts off, render callbacks absent, target updates, and cancellation. This is not live in-game verification.

FallingCoconut observations with numeric Combo = 0 are treated as private initial drops. A configured spawning account catches its own 0x drop locally, without waiting for a coordinator assignment. If that private event omits an owner, the receiving account is used, based on the private-drop behavior reported by the user; ordinary shared drops never infer an owner this way. A new 0x event does not inherit stale chain ownership. Other local group selectors exclude private drops. Alt private catches remain advertised as available so the existing coordinator can observe the owner's first touch; subsequent shared alt drops use the explicit-stack solo reservation rules above, with relay assignment as the fallback for missing counts. The supplied live trace confirms that Combo is the stack count and ComboOwner is the Player owner; Points is a separate value and stays at 100 in that trace.

Regression tests cover 0x and six shared drops, main-only odd-stack acceptance across roles, stale-job rejection, nearby spawner clearance/hold, immediate main and owner turns, and the production Atlas collector receiving a reserved main turn with no relay assignment. Own-main help is tested over six same-field drops and a field change. These are isolated tests, not a live multi-account soak test; all team instances must reload the updated client for shared reservations to apply consistently. No coordinator update is required.

Live troubleshooting: Coconut Team → Copy Combo Diagnostics exports the last 40 scalar-only FX, parsed-target, and decision entries plus role/field settings. It excludes coordinator URL/key and full configuration, does not retain Instances or raw payload tables, and clears on unload. Use matching exports from the main and a spawning alt after several shared drops to verify actual ownership, stack counts, field resets, and decisions. The supplied live trace established the ComboOwner/Combo/Points schema mismatch; a sanitized copy is now replayed by tests. Live multi-account catch success still needs verification.

Travel safety: normal tween and graph task travel acquire a combo movement lock before starting. An active combo session is stopped and restored before the task creates travel constraints. While the lock, a normal tween constraint, fast travel, or an existing movement tween is active, combo selection/walking is deferred; normal task constraints are not destroyed by combo PauseOthers. The lock remains held through the normal final landing wait and releases on completion/error, with dead-thread/character-change pruning for cancellation. Fresh live targets can resume after safe arrival. Tests cover in-flight constraint preservation, the final fall, successful resumption, errors, and canceled travel ownership. Coordinator code is unchanged.

The travel regression also covers a synchronous ownership race: finishing a combo resumes a queued task, which may acquire travel ownership before the next combo Begin call finishes. Begin now rechecks the lock after Finish and leaves that task's newly created constraints intact. Live-trace replay checks the configured 5-drop main-help interval with Points fixed at 100, then replays the same schema under a spawning-alt owner to verify main-only odd-stack selection.
